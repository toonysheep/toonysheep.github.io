I am a doctoral student in Management Science and Engineering at the [School of Management, Huazhong University of Science and Technology](https://english.cm.hust.edu.cn/). My research focuses on planning for planetary rovers, including hierarchical task network planning, reinforcement learning, and integrated task and motion planning.

## Education

All three stages of study are at the School of Management, Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

- **2023–present** · Doctoral studies in Management Science and Engineering
- **2021–2023** · Master’s studies
- **2017–2021** · Undergraduate studies

## Research Outputs

- **Li, C.**, Yang, J., and Qi, C. “An Integrated Hierarchical Task Network and Deep Reinforcement Learning Method for Cooperative Multi-Rover Mission Planning.” 3rd Aerospace Frontier Conference (AFC 2026), Nanjing, China, 2026.
- **Li, C.**, Luo, L., and Qi, C. “Lunar South Pole Rover Path Planning Considering Time-Varying Illumination and Shadows.” 20th Annual Meeting of the Deep Space Exploration Technical Committee, Chinese Society of Astronautics, Kunming, China, 28–31 August 2024 (in Chinese).
- **Li, C.**, Luo, L., and Qi, C. “Lunar Rover Path Planning for South Polar Region with Time-variant Illumination.” *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence* (under review).
